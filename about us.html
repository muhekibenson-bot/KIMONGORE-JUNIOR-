Our Story

the school is located in Rukureijo village, Kyamusoon parish,Ruborogota sub-county in Isingiro District, along Kikagate kitwe Ntugamo Road.

The school was named in honour of Late Can.Rev.Kimongore and has grown over the years to become the best school with the best performance for the surrounding communities.it has employed more and more staff from the community and with 1000 children in enrolment.

we have a centre number and operates with in laws established by the government of Uganda.

Our Mission

To provide affordable, accessible and quality education to the people of the district and the surrounding communities, with compassion and dedication services.
